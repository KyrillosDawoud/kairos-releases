Install Kairos

  1. Open the latest release and download the file that ends in x64-setup.exe.
  2. Open it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway. Windows shows this because the installer is not code signed yet.
  3. Kairos installs for your Windows account only and needs no administrator rights. It runs on Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.

A versioned release checks for updates and installs signed updates on its own. Settings, About shows your version and has Check for updates.

Disconnect a service

Each service has a sheet in Connections with one way to disconnect. Disconnecting removes Kairos's access from your computer. Some services also keep an approval on their side, which you can remove there.

Google Calendar

Connections, Google Calendar, Disconnect. Kairos deletes the token from Windows Credential Manager and revokes its access at Google. To check, see Revoke Google access.

To stop one Google service and keep the rest, open Connections, Google and turn off Tasks, Drive or Classroom. Kairos stops reading it at once. Your tasks stay in Kairos and in Google Tasks.

Outlook and Microsoft 365

To stop one service, such as Mail or Teams chat: Connections, Microsoft, turn its switch off. Kairos stops reading it at once. To end the whole connection: Connections, Microsoft, Disconnect. Kairos deletes the token from your computer. Then remove the approval at Microsoft:

  • A personal Microsoft account: open account.live.com/consent/Manage, choose Kairos, then remove its permissions.
  • A work or school account: ask your IT team to remove Kairos. Your organization manages these approvals.

Canvas

Connections, Canvas, Disconnect. Kairos deletes your school's session or access token from your computer. If you used an access token, also delete it in Canvas: Account, Settings, Approved Integrations.

Banks through Plaid

Connections, Banks through Plaid, then Remove next to the bank. Kairos ends the link at Plaid and deletes it from Kairos's server. You can also see and remove every app connected through Plaid at my.plaid.com.

Banks through SimpleFIN

Connections, Banks through SimpleFIN, Disconnect. Kairos deletes the access address from your computer. Then remove Kairos in your account at SimpleFIN Bridge.

Calendar feeds

Open Calendar and remove the linked calendar from the calendar's sidebar. Kairos deletes the address and its copy of the calendar.

Revoke Google access

Disconnecting Google Calendar in Kairos revokes access for you. To check or do it yourself, at any time and from any device:

  1. Open myaccount.google.com/permissions and sign in to the Google account you connected.
  2. Choose Kairos in the list of third-party apps.
  3. Remove Kairos's access.

Kairos then says Google Calendar needs you to connect again, and stops reading it. If you signed in to Kairos with Google, that sign-in appears on the same page. Removing it there does not delete your Kairos account. See Delete your account for that.

Turn off Claude

Settings, Claude, Remove key. Kairos deletes the key from your computer and stops sending anything to Anthropic. To stop the key working everywhere, delete it in your Anthropic Console as well.

Kairos takes a picture of your screen only with a question you ask: when you hold Ctrl Alt Space to ask the companion, turn on Look at my screen or press Ctrl Alt S. One picture goes with that one question and is never saved. A question to the companion also says which app was in front and its window title, and only with that picture. Kairos takes no picture of a password manager or a sign in page. Settings, Companion, Read my screen when I ask turns it off, and then Kairos takes no picture at all.

Forget what Kairos learned

Settings, What Kairos has learned lists every item and memory with its evidence. Remove deletes one item. Forget everything clears all of it. The switch turns learning off. All of it lives on your computer only.

Sign out

Settings, Account, Sign out. Kairos ends the session on this computer and removes its cached copy of your Kairos data. Your connections stay on the computer until you disconnect them.

Delete your account

In Kairos, Settings, Account, Delete account. Kairos ends every bank link at Plaid, deletes your account with everything Kairos's server holds for it, including what Kairos remembers about you, disconnects Google, Outlook, Canvas and SimpleFIN Bridge and clears what it keeps for you on that computer, your conversations with Kairos included.

If you cannot open Kairos, write to privacy@YOUR-DOMAIN from the email address on your Kairos account, with the subject "Delete my Kairos account". Within 30 days we end every bank link at Plaid and delete your account with everything Kairos's server holds for it. We write back when it is done.

To clear another computer as well:

  1. Windows Settings, Apps, Installed apps, Kairos, Uninstall. If the uninstaller offers to delete the app's data, choose it.
  2. Open Credential Manager from the Start menu, then Windows Credentials. Remove any entry that names app.kairos.desktop.

Anthropic keeps requests under its own policy, whether they went through Kairos AI or your own key. See Claude and Anthropic.

Contact

Write to privacy@YOUR-DOMAIN. Include your Kairos version from Settings, About and what you saw on screen. Never send a password, a token, a setup token or an API key. We will never ask for one.
