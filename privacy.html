Privacy policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

Kairos is a desktop app for Windows that brings your calendars, classes, money and tasks into one place. This policy says what Kairos reads, where it keeps it, who else sees it and how to delete it. It covers the Kairos app, Kairos's server and this website.

The short version

  • Kairos reads only the services you connect, and reads them only to show them to you.
  • Access to Google, Outlook, Canvas and SimpleFIN Bridge stays on your computer, in Windows Credential Manager.
  • Kairos's server keeps your account, the tasks, events and notes you make in Kairos, what Kairos remembers about you and the links to banks you connect through Plaid.
  • Your data goes to Anthropic only when you ask Kairos something: through Kairos AI on Kairos's server, or through your own Anthropic API key if you add one.
  • Your conversations with Kairos stay on your computer.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads or use your data to train AI models.
  • You can disconnect any service at any time and ask us to delete your account.

Who makes Kairos

Kairos is made by YOUR NAME OR COMPANY ("we" in this policy). Write to privacy@YOUR-DOMAIN with any question about your data.

Your Kairos account

You sign in to Kairos with an email address and password, or with your Google or Microsoft account.

  • Email and password. Kairos's server stores your email address and a one-way hash of your password. Nobody can read the password back.
  • Google or Microsoft. You sign in on Google's or Microsoft's own page. Kairos asks only for basic sign-in information: your email address and, where the provider shares them, your name and profile picture. Kairos's server stores these with your account. Signing in this way does not give Kairos your calendar or mail. Those are separate connections that you turn on yourself.
  • Profile. Your display name and time zone.

Your session is kept in Windows Credential Manager, so Kairos signs you in on the next start without asking.

What Kairos's server stores

Kairos's server runs on Supabase. It stores:

  • Your account, as described above.
  • What you make in Kairos: your tasks, the events you create in Kairos, your notes, your Kairos calendars and the links between them.
  • What Kairos remembers about you: short sentences you add in Settings, Memory, and the ones Kairos saves as you talk, such as a goal or a routine, so it knows you on every device. You can edit or forget each one.
  • For each bank you connect through Plaid: the item id Plaid gives it, the bank's name, the access token Plaid issues for it and the last error code Plaid reported. Only Kairos's server function can read the access token. The app never receives it.
  • For each Kairos AI request, a usage record: which part of Kairos asked, the model, the number of tokens and the cost. Not the request and not the answer.

Each account's rows are protected by row level security, so only your signed-in account can read them. Events from Google Calendar, Outlook and calendar feeds, your Canvas data, your balances and transactions, your conversations with Kairos and your Anthropic key are not stored on the server. Balances and transactions from Plaid, and Kairos AI requests and answers, pass through the server and are not saved there.

Like any web service, Supabase keeps request logs, including IP addresses, for a short time to run and protect the service.

What stays on your computer

Most of what Kairos reads never leaves your PC.

In Windows Credential Manager

Windows encrypts these for your Windows account:

  • Your Kairos session.
  • The refresh tokens for Google Calendar and Outlook.
  • Your Canvas sign-in session or access token.
  • Your SimpleFIN Bridge access address.
  • The addresses of calendar feeds you link.
  • Your Anthropic API key. The app can save or remove it but cannot read it back.

In the app's storage

  • Caches of your events, tasks, connected calendars, Canvas work and money, so Kairos opens fast and works offline. Money is read again at most every 6 hours unless you choose Refresh.
  • What Kairos learns from how you use it: when you finish tasks, which tasks move to a later day, how long study blocks run, which suggestions you act on and which proposals from Claude you apply. Kairos keeps at most 3,000 of these signals, none older than 180 days. They are never sent to Kairos's server.
  • Your conversations with Kairos, the newest 50, with the files you attached, so you can reopen one where it ended. You can delete any of them, or all of them, on the Kairos screen.
  • Money category corrections, your net worth history, the names, order and notes you give your accounts, which accounts you hid, class times and settings.
  • Company icons on Money. Kairos asks each bank's and merchant's own website for its icon, the way a browser does, and keeps the answer for 30 days. That site learns only that a computer asked for its icon. No icon service is involved. Settings, Money, Show logos turns it off.

Signing out removes the cached copy of your Kairos data from the computer. Connections stay until you disconnect them. Settings, What Kairos has learned lets you remove any learned item, forget everything or turn learning off.

Reminders and the morning brief are made on your computer from these caches. They use Windows notifications, not a push service.

What each connection reads

Every connection is optional and turned on by you. All of them are read only except Google Calendar, where Kairos can create, change and delete events on calendars you can edit, when you ask it to. Kairos uses what it reads only to show it to you in Kairos and, when you ask, to answer with Claude.

Google Calendar

Reads
The list of calendars you can see and their events: title, time, location, description and video call link. Your email address, to show which account is connected.
Changes
When you ask, Kairos creates, changes or deletes an event in Google Calendar: when you save an event, drag or resize it, delete it, add one with quick add, or apply a change Kairos proposed. Only on calendars you own or can edit, and only the event you changed. Kairos never changes a calendar you can only view and never emails your guests about a change.
Access
openid, email, calendar.calendarlist.readonly and calendar.events. The last one lets Kairos read events and create, change and delete them. It does not let Kairos change your calendars, their sharing or your settings.
Kept
The refresh token in Credential Manager. Events in the app's cache on your computer.
Ends
Disconnect deletes the token and revokes Kairos's access at Google.

Outlook and Microsoft 365

Reads
Your calendar events: subject, time, location, the first lines of the body and the online meeting link. Your email address.
Access
openid, email, offline_access, User.Read and Calendars.Read. Kairos cannot read your mail or change your calendar.
Kept
The refresh token in Credential Manager. Events in the app's cache on your computer.
Ends
Disconnect deletes the token. Remove the approval at Microsoft as well.

Canvas

Reads
Your current courses with their sections, teachers and your scores, assignments with your submissions, course files and modules, announcements and your planner. Only from the one school you connect.
Access
You sign in to your school's own Canvas page in a Kairos window, or paste a Canvas access token where your school allows them. Kairos only reads. It never submits, posts or changes anything.
Kept
The school's session cookies or the token in Credential Manager. Your Canvas data in the app's cache on your computer.
Ends
Disconnect deletes the session or token from your computer.

University of Virginia class search

Reads
The meeting days and times of your courses, from the university's public class search.
Sends
Only a course's subject and number, such as ECON 2010. Nothing about you.
Kept
The answer, on your computer, refreshed at most once a day.

Banks through Plaid

Reads
Your accounts and balances and up to 90 days of transactions, with the category your bank or Plaid gives each one.
Access
You sign in to your bank on Plaid's page in your browser. Kairos never sees your bank password. Plaid handles your data under the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.
Kept
The link to each bank on Kairos's server, as described above. Balances and transactions in the app's cache on your computer.
Ends
Remove ends the link at Plaid and deletes it from Kairos's server.

Banks through SimpleFIN Bridge

Reads
Your accounts and balances and up to 90 days of transactions, including pending ones.
Access
You connect your banks at SimpleFIN Bridge and paste a one-time setup token into Kairos.
Kept
The access address in Credential Manager. Balances and transactions in the app's cache on your computer. Nothing on Kairos's server.
Ends
Disconnect deletes the access address. Remove Kairos at SimpleFIN Bridge as well.

Calendar feeds

Reads
The calendar at an iCal address you paste, such as a published Outlook or iCloud calendar.
Kept
The address in Credential Manager. The calendar in the app's cache on your computer.

Voice and reading aloud

Voice
The microphone in Ask Kairos uses the Windows speech recognizer. Kairos sends no audio anywhere. If Windows' Online speech recognition setting is on, Windows processes your speech under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
Aloud
Answers read aloud use the voices installed in Windows, on your computer.

Claude and Anthropic

Kairos's assistant runs on Claude, made by Anthropic, in one of two ways:

  • Kairos AI, when you are signed in. A request goes from your computer to Kairos's server, which checks your session and sends it to Anthropic under Kairos's own Anthropic account, then streams the answer back. The server keeps only the usage record described above. Each account can use up to $3 of Kairos AI a month.
  • Your own key, if you add an Anthropic API key in Settings. It is kept in Credential Manager and sent only to Anthropic's Messages API. Each request goes from your computer straight to Anthropic, under your own Anthropic account, and Kairos's server is not involved.

When you ask Kairos something, it sends:

  • Your question and, in a conversation, the earlier questions and answers.
  • Your events for the next 14 days: title, time, calendar name and location.
  • Your open tasks with their due dates.
  • Canvas work due in the next 14 days, with its class and whether you submitted it.
  • What Kairos remembers about you, the patterns it measured in your calendar and tasks and what it has learned about your schedule.
  • For a planning question, the free time Kairos found in your calendar.
  • On the Kairos screen, more when a question needs it: other days of your calendar, tasks, search results from your events, tasks and notes, class work and, for a money question, money as described below.

Only in these cases, and only for that request:

  • Money goes only with a question about money: net worth, this month and last month, accounts and balances, recurring charges, charges worth a look and up to 150 transactions from the last 60 days.
  • A picture of your screen goes only when you turn on Look at my screen or press Ctrl Alt S: one picture of the screen under your pointer, scaled down and sent with that question. It is never saved to disk.
  • A syllabus goes when you import one: the PDF you pick, the course name and the term dates.
  • The morning brief, if you turn on the brief by Claude: today's events, tasks due or overdue, what needs you and your focus hours.
  • Files you attach: the images and PDFs you add to a message on the Kairos screen, with that message and the later ones in the same conversation.
  • Web search and web pages: Claude may search the web and read a page through Anthropic to answer a question.
  • After a conversation, unless learning is off: what each side said, once more, so Claude can update what Kairos remembers about you. Files and tool results are not sent again.

Claude proposes changes. Nothing in Kairos changes until you apply them, and Claude can never change events that come from Google, Outlook or a feed.

Anthropic handles these requests under its own terms with you. By Anthropic's published policy, it does not use inputs and outputs from its API to train its models by default, and it deletes them within 30 days, with the exceptions it lists. See Is my data used for model training? and How long do you store my organization's data? Kairos does not send your data to any other AI provider. If that changes, this policy will name the provider first, and Settings, Kairos AI will show it.

Google user data

Kairos's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The use of information received from Google Workspace scopes will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular:

  • What Kairos reads. With Google Calendar: your calendar list and events, as listed above. With Sign in with Google: your email address, name and profile picture.
  • What Kairos changes. With Google Calendar: the events you create, change or delete in Kairos, on calendars you can edit, only when you ask. Nothing else.
  • How it is used. Only to show your calendars in the calendar, reminders, Today and Up next, and to make the changes you ask for. When you ask Kairos a question, also to answer it. Your sign-in details identify your Kairos account.
  • Where it is kept. Calendar data stays on your computer, in the app's cache and in your conversations with Kairos, and is never stored on Kairos's server. Sign-in details are stored with your account on Kairos's server.
  • Who it goes to. Calendar data goes to Anthropic only when you ask Kairos a question, as part of that request, through Kairos AI or your own key. Kairos AI passes it through Kairos's server without saving it. It goes to no one else.
  • Never. Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to decide credit or lending, never given to data brokers and never used to create, train or improve any AI or machine learning model.
  • People. Nobody reads your Google data. The only exceptions: you ask us to in a support request, security needs it (for example, to investigate abuse) or the law requires it.
  • Deleting it. Disconnect Google Calendar in Kairos to delete the token and revoke access, or remove Kairos at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Signing out removes the cached events.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not show ads or use your data for advertising.
  • We do not share your data with data brokers or use it to decide credit or lending.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models, ours or anyone else's.
  • Kairos never moves money, makes a payment or changes anything at your bank.
  • Kairos has no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • Nobody at YOUR NAME OR COMPANY reads your data unless you ask us to in a support request, it is needed for security or the law requires it.

Who else handles your data

Kairos relies on these services. Each one receives only what its part needs.

  • Supabase runs Kairos's server: your account, sign-in emails and the data listed under What Kairos's server stores.
  • Anthropic runs Claude, only when you ask: under Kairos's Anthropic account for Kairos AI, or under your own if you add your key.
  • Plaid and SimpleFIN Bridge connect your banks, if you use them.
  • Google, Microsoft and Instructure (Canvas, through your school) provide the data you connect and the sign-ins you choose.
  • GitHub hosts the installer, the updates Kairos checks for and this website. Like any web host, it sees your IP address when you download or Kairos checks for an update.

We may share data when the law requires it or to protect the safety of people or the service. If Kairos is ever sold or merged, we will ask for your consent before Google user data moves to the new owner.

These services may process data in the United States and in other countries.

Keeping and deleting data

  • On your computer. Caches last until you sign out, disconnect the service or uninstall Kairos. Learned signals are dropped after 180 days. Conversations with Kairos last until you delete them, and only the newest 50 are kept.
  • On the server. Your account and what you make in Kairos are kept while your account exists. When you delete a task, event or note, Kairos marks it deleted and hides it everywhere. It is removed for good when your account is deleted. A memory you forget is deleted at once. Kairos AI usage records are kept while your account exists.
  • Plaid links. Kept until you remove the bank or your account is deleted.
  • At Anthropic. Under Anthropic's policy, linked above.

To delete your account, open Kairos, Settings, Account, Delete account. Kairos ends every bank link at Plaid, deletes your account with everything Kairos's server holds for it, disconnects your connections and clears what it keeps for you on that computer. You can also write to privacy@YOUR-DOMAIN from the email address on the account, and we do the same within 30 days and write back when it is done. Support says how to clear another computer.

Security

  • Every connection uses HTTPS.
  • Tokens and keys on your computer are kept in Windows Credential Manager, which Windows encrypts for your Windows account. They are never written to a file in plain text.
  • Kairos's server never holds your access to Google, Microsoft, Canvas or SimpleFIN Bridge. Plaid access tokens are readable only by Kairos's server function.
  • Supabase encrypts the database at rest. Row level security keeps each account's rows private to that account.
  • Updates are signed, and Kairos installs only an update whose signature checks out.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will tell you without undue delay.
