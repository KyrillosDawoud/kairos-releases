Your data

As little as it can, as close to you as it can.

  • Sign-ins stay on your computer.

    Access to Google, Canvas and SimpleFIN Bridge is kept in Windows Credential Manager on your PC. Kairos's server never holds it.

  • The server keeps what you make.

    Your account, tasks, events, notes, the applications and contacts you track and what Kairos remembers about you sync through Kairos's server, readable only by your account. So does the link to each bank you connect through Plaid. Your conversations with Kairos stay on your computer.

  • Claude reads only when you ask.

    Your data goes to Anthropic only when you ask Kairos something, through Kairos AI or your own key. A picture of your screen goes only with a question you ask about it, one picture for that question, and never once you turn screen reading off.

  • Nothing sold. No ads.

    Kairos does not sell your data, show ads or use your data to train AI models. The app and this site have no analytics or trackers.
