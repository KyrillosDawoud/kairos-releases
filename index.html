What Kairos does

One window for the day ahead. Kairos reads the places your life already lives and shows what is next.

Calendar

Every calendar in one view.

Day, week, month and agenda. Google Calendar works both ways: create, move or delete an event in Kairos and it changes in Google. Outlook and any iCal link sit beside your own events, read only. Type "study Thursday 7 to 9" and Kairos puts it at the right time.

  • Drag to move, drag the edge to resize
  • A reminder before each event, 10 minutes by default
  • D, W, M and A switch the view

Money

Your money, read only.

Connect banks and cards through Plaid or SimpleFIN Bridge. Kairos shows net worth, spending against last month by the same day, bills coming up and charges worth a look. Every category says why it is there.

  • One correction moves every charge from that merchant
  • Totals are computed in exact cents
  • Kairos never moves money or makes a payment

Ask Kairos

Ask in plain words.

Ctrl K searches your events, tasks and notes, or makes a new one from one line. Kairos answers from your calendar, tasks, classes and money with Claude, remembers what matters to you and proposes changes. Nothing changes until you apply them.

  • Ctrl Alt K opens it from any app
  • Speak instead of typing, through Windows speech recognition
  • Claude reads money only when you ask about money

Connections

Each connection asks first.

Every service has a page that says what Kairos can do with it and what it never does, before you connect. Disconnect any time and Kairos removes the access from your computer.

  • Sign in at Google, Microsoft or your school, never in a Kairos form
  • Access stays in Windows Credential Manager
  • Read only, except the Google Calendar events you change

Your data

Kairos keeps as little as it can, as close to you as it can.

  • Sign-ins stay on your computer.

    Access to Google, Outlook, Canvas and SimpleFIN Bridge is kept in Windows Credential Manager on your PC. Kairos's server never holds it.

  • The server keeps what you make.

    Your account, tasks, events, notes and what Kairos remembers about you sync through Kairos's server, readable only by your account. So does the link to each bank you connect through Plaid. Your conversations with Kairos stay on your computer.

  • Claude reads only when you ask.

    Your data goes to Anthropic only when you ask Kairos something, through Kairos AI or your own key. A picture of your screen goes only when you ask about the screen.

  • Nothing sold. No ads.

    Kairos does not sell your data, show ads or use your data to train AI models. The app and this site have no analytics or trackers.
